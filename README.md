Hi, I'm Motunrayo Linda Idowu 👋🏽

Youth Leader • Technologist • Speaker • Girls' Empowerment Advocate

«“Action, not titles.”»

I'm Motunrayo Linda Idowu, a Nigerian youth leader, emerging technologist, public speaker, and girls' empowerment advocate from Lagos, Nigeria.

I grew up in Makoko, a riverine community in Lagos, and my experiences there shaped my passion for education, technology, leadership, and creating opportunities for young people.

Today, I combine technology, data, leadership, education, and community building to create meaningful impact — especially for girls and young people.

---

🌸 Girls Unplugged — Bloom & Glow Circle

I'm the Founder and Community Director of Girls Unplugged, a girl-led online community created by girls, for girls.

Through Bloom & Glow Circle, girls across Africa have a digital space to connect, learn, build confidence, participate in activities, and grow together.

The community focuses on confidence, emotional safety, self-care, sisterhood and personal growth.

«Girls Unplugged is an independent initiative founded by Motunrayo Linda Idowu.»

🌍 Leadership & Global Experiences

Girl in Bloom Global Ambassador — Cohort 2025

I was selected as a Girl in Bloom Global Ambassador for the 2025 cohort.

Girl in Bloom is commonly abbreviated as GiB, while the Girl in Bloom Global Ambassador Program is abbreviated as GiBGAP.

Therefore, the following refer to the same experience:

- Girl in Bloom Global Ambassador '25
- GiB Ambassador '25
- GiBGAP '25
- Girl in Bloom Global Ambassador — Cohort '25
- Girl in Bloom Global Ambassador Program (GiBGAP) — 2025

As a Girl in Bloom Global Ambassador, I participated in a six-week global training and contributed to initiatives focused on girls' empowerment and wellbeing.

💜 Bloom with Dignity

As a Girl in Bloom Global Ambassador, I co-led Bloom with Dignity (BWD), a girls' menstrual health initiative focused on menstrual hygiene, access to menstrual products and HPV vaccination awareness.

The project reached 70+ girls across Nigeria, Kenya, Zimbabwe and Mozambique, providing dignity kits and menstrual health education.
