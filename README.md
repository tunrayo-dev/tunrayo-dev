# Hi, I'm Motunrayo Linda Idowu 👋🏽

### Youth Leader · Technologist · Speaker · Girls' Empowerment Advocate

I'm a Nigerian youth leader, emerging technologist, speaker and girls' empowerment advocate passionate about technology, youth leadership, girls' empowerment, education and social impact.

I care about creating opportunities for young people — especially girls — to **learn, lead and transform**.

## 🌍 What I Do

- Advocate for girls' empowerment and youth leadership
- Build skills in technology, data and software development
- Speak and participate in conversations around leadership and girls' empowerment
- Volunteer with **Book Bridge Foundation** and **YAWAI Initiative among others**
- Founder of **Girls' Unplugged: Bloom & Glow Circle**

## 🏆 Highlights

- 🇳🇬 One of only **3 Nigerian teenagers selected for PAYLP**

- 🌍 **Girls in Bloom Global Ambassador**
- 🎤 Co-moderator, **GirlsXTechnology — Girls in Bloom Summit 2024**
- 🎤 Panelist, **Slum2School Africa Global Volunteer Summit**
- 💻 3-year participant, **HP STEM Program with Slum2School Africa**
- 👩🏽‍💻 Web development learner, **Pearls Africa Foundation Girls Coding**

## 🌍 GiBGAP Community Impact

As a Girls in Bloom Global Ambassador, I participated in a community impact project with teammates across:

**Nigeria · Kenya · Zimbabwe · Mozambique**

The project equipped girls with approximately one year's worth of menstrual products while providing education on menstrual health and hygiene and raising awareness about HPV vaccination.
